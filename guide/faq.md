> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

## Does it cost anything?

No. The companion is free, and so is everything it shows except the faction-level parts
marked **Pro** on their pages.

## Is it allowed by Torn?

It's written to follow Torn's scripting rules: it reads only pages you open, does at most
one game action per click, never submits for you, and never loads pages in the background.
See [What it never does](what-it-never-does.md).

## Does it need my Torn API key?

No. It uses your torn-tracker.com [script key](website:script-key.md), which isn't a Torn
API key.

## Do I have to share anything?

No. Every kind of sharing starts off, and the features that show data work without you
sharing anything. See [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md).

## Can people see what I shared?

The site never publishes who sent anything, except that a player can see who reported
their own loadout.

## Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in Torn PDA. See [Install in Torn PDA](install-pda.md).

## Where's the code?

On GitHub, under the GPLv3. The [Credits](settings-panel.md#the-sections) section of the settings lists every script
it took an idea from.
