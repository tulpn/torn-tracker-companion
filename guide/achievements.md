> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your [script key](website:script-key.md).

## What you see

When torn-tracker.com says you've earned an achievement, a small toast shows in the corner
of the Torn page you're on (at the bottom on a phone): **Achievement unlocked**, its name,
its tier if it has one, and one sentence on what you did. A hidden achievement says
**Hidden achievement found**. If you earned several at once, it names the first and says
how many more.

The toast goes by itself after a few seconds. It stays while your pointer or focus is on
it, and **✕** closes it. **See it and share** opens your achievements on torn-tracker.com.

Your **featured** achievements, the ones you picked for your card, show at the top of the
companion's [settings page](settings-panel.md).

## Switch them off

1. Open the [settings](settings-panel.md) and go to **General**.
2. Under **Achievements**, switch off the toast, the featured ones, or both.
3. Press **Save**.

With both off, the companion stops asking torn-tracker.com about achievements.

## Sharing is always your own press

Nothing about your achievements is posted from the companion. **Share** is on
torn-tracker.com, and only ever happens when you press it there.

> **What it reads and sends.** When you open a Torn page, and at most once every five
> minutes, the companion asks torn-tracker.com, with your script key, for the achievements
> you earned since it last asked. It sends nothing else. It never asks while you're on the
> attack page or the tab is in the background, and the very first time it only notes the
> time, so a new install doesn't show old achievements. It keeps your featured
> achievements and when it last asked on this device; [Forget me](forget-me.md) clears
> them.

## If nothing shows

Check your script key under **Keys** in the settings. Achievements are worked out on
torn-tracker.com from what it already knows, so one can take a few minutes to arrive.
