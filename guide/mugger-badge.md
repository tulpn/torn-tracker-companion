> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key.

## What you see

Some buyers mug a seller shortly after buying from them, while the money from the sale is
still on hand. Sellers who were mugged like this can report it on torn-tracker.com. When
at least three different sellers, from at least two factions, reported the same player in
the last 30 days, that player is listed.

On a listed player's profile and on the attack page against them, a badge beside the name
says so:

> Mugged sellers: 3 in 30 days

Hover over it for the counts over 30 and 90 days and when they were last reported. Click
it to open torn-tracker.com's seller-safety page. A player who isn't listed gets no badge,
and no badge isn't a guarantee: bank your cash before you sell either way.

The badge states what was reported. It never shows who reported it. Reports older than 90
days stop counting, so a player who stops drops off the list.

Switch it off in the [settings](settings-panel.md): **After-sale mug badge**, under
**Market and trading**.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the id of the player whose profile or attack page
> you opened and sends it to torn-tracker.com with your script key, to ask whether they
> are listed. Each answer is kept for 10 minutes. Nothing is shared about you or them.
