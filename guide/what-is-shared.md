> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Seeing the data never needs you to share any.

> **What it sends.** Only what Torn showed you on a page you opened yourself, with the
> time you saw it, to torn-tracker.com with your script key. Nothing from Torn's company
> or faction application pages is ever shared. There, Applicant badges sends torn-tracker.com
> only the applicants' player ids and which list it is, to show their job-board cards.

## Switch on what you want

1. Open the settings (the **Torn Tracker** link in the sidebar) and go to **Sharing**.
2. Read the notice at the top, and the line beside each kind saying what it sends.
3. Switch on the kinds you're happy to share.
4. Each switch saves as you tick it, and a ticked kind is shared from then on: ticking it is
   your agreement to its line. The line under the switches says whether sharing is on.

Every kind is explained in [Sharing kinds, one by one](sharing-kinds.md).

## Seeing what goes

Under the switches, a queue line shows how many reports are waiting, when the last upload
went and what torn-tracker.com answered. For more, switch on **Log what the script does to
the browser console** under **General**: the console then says, for every report, why it was
or wasn't shared (a kind that's off, an item the site didn't ask for, a repeat within the
window), and what each upload sent and got back. Your keys are never written there.

## Why share

Torn's API doesn't publish foreign stock, a player's bazaar, live auctions or loadouts.
Players who share what their pages show let torn-tracker.com publish it openly, for every
player and tool.

## Who can see that you shared

The script key tells torn-tracker.com who sent each report, so it can catch false ones.
The site never publishes who sent anything, with one exception: **a player can see who
reported their own loadout.**

## Changing your mind

Switch a kind off at any time: it stops being shared from then on. If
the explanation of what a kind sends changes in an update, sharing pauses until you've
read the new one and pressed **Share the kinds I picked**, the button that then appears
under the switches.
