> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Seeing the data never needs you to share any.

> **What it sends.** Only what Torn showed you on a page you opened yourself, with the
> time you saw it, to torn-tracker.com with your script key. Nothing is ever sent from
> Torn's company or faction application pages.

## Switch on what you want

1. Open the panel and go to **Sharing**.
2. Read the notice at the top, and the line beside each kind saying what it sends.
3. Switch on the kinds you're happy to share.
4. Press **Share the kinds I picked**. Until you do, nothing is sent.

Every kind is explained in [Sharing kinds, one by one](sharing-kinds.md).

## Why share

Torn's API doesn't publish foreign stock, a player's bazaar, live auctions or loadouts.
Players who share what their pages show let torn-tracker.com publish it openly, for every
player and tool.

## Who can see that you shared

The script key tells torn-tracker.com who sent each report, so it can catch false ones.
The site never publishes who sent anything, with one exception: **a player can see who
reported their own loadout.**

## Changing your mind

Switch a kind off at any time and press **Save**: it stops being shared from then on. If
the explanation of what a kind sends changes in an update, sharing pauses until you've
read the new one and pressed **Share the kinds I picked** again.
