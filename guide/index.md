> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone.

The companion is one userscript for Torn. It shows torn-tracker.com's data where you make
the decision (fair-fight badges beside players, your faction's dibs on the war page, the
best buy when you land abroad) and adds small helpers to pages you already use. If you
choose to, it also shares what those pages show you, so the site can publish what Torn's
API doesn't: foreign stock, bazaar and auction listings, loadouts.

## Start here

1. [Install it on desktop](install-desktop.md) or [in Torn PDA](install-pda.md).
2. Paste your [script key](website:script-key.md) into the panel's **Keys** tab.
3. [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md). Everything starts off.

## What it never does

It reads only pages you open yourself, never loads Torn pages in the background, never
submits a form or presses a game button for you, and talks to two hosts only:
torn-tracker.com and, if a feature ever needs it, Torn's own API. The full list is in
[What it never does](what-it-never-does.md).

## Open source

The script is free software under the GPLv3, and its code is public on GitHub. Every
script it took an idea from is credited in the panel's **Credits** tab.
