> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key, and you need to be the verified
director or recruiter on torn-tracker.com.

## What you see

When you open your company's or your faction's application list in Torn, each applicant
who shared a card on the torn-tracker.com job board, and applied to you, gets a badge:
**10 merits · 1,234 job points · good fit**. The badge links to their full card on the
site. Applicants without a shared card get nothing.

torn-tracker.com works out whose board it is from your verified role, never from the page,
so it only ever answers for your own company or faction.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the applicants' profile links on the list and asks
> torn-tracker.com for their shared cards. Nothing from application pages is ever shared,
> whatever you have switched on, and the companion never accepts, declines or clicks.
