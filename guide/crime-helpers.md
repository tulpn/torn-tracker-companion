> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Morale and burglary need no key; value per nerve and sharing
outcomes need your script key.

## Morale

Torn doesn't show your crime morale. The companion reads it from what the crimes page
loaded and shows it in the header: **Morale 87%**.

## Value per nerve

Beside each crime option, a line like **$311/nerve** shows the average money and item
value players got from it, for each nerve it costs. It comes from torn-tracker.com, worked
out from players' shared outcomes, and shows only once at least 20 players have reported
that crime. Hover it to see how many, and how often it succeeds.

## Burglary targets

Each burglary target shows how long it has left before it disappears: red at 12 hours or
less, amber under 3 days, green beyond. Case the ones about to go first.

## Share your outcomes

If you switch **Your crime outcomes** on in [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md), each
crime you commit sends which crime it was, how it went and what you got. torn-tracker.com
keeps yours for your own view, and publishes only totals per crime once at least 20
players have reported it. Those totals are what the value-per-nerve lines are made of.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads what the crimes page loaded, and asks
> torn-tracker.com for the crime's value per nerve. Only with Your crime outcomes on, it
> sends your own attempts' results. Nothing is clicked.
