> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. No key needed.

## What you see

Torn's jail page doesn't say how hard a bust is. Beside each prisoner, the companion shows
the community's rule of thumb: **level × (hours left + 3)**, the score TornTools uses. Lower
is easier. It's green up to 100, red from 250, and amber in between; you can change both
numbers in the settings, under **Features**. Hover the score to see how it was worked out.

It's a rule of thumb, **not Torn's odds**: your own chance also depends on your skill and
perks, which the page doesn't show.

## What it never does

It never presses **Bust** or **Bail**, and never changes a bust link to skip Torn's
confirmation. You bust as you always do.

## Sharing (off unless you switch it on)

With **Jail difficulty bands** switched on in [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md),
each prisoner's level band (like 26-50) and time-left band (like 1-3 hours) is sent, never
who they are, so torn-tracker.com can count prisoners per band.
