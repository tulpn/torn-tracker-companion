> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key.

## Energy to the next gym

Beside the gym you're working towards, the companion shows **about N energy to go (an
estimate)**. It's an estimate because the game counts gym experience, not energy.

## What makes it work

Switch on **Your gym progress** and **Your gym trains** in
[Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md). The companion then sends how far into the gym
you are and how much each train raised your stat. These are **only for your own gym view**,
on torn-tracker.com and in the bot's [gym command](bot:gym.md): never shown to anyone
else, never published, not even in totals, and kept 30 days.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the gym page and the page's own answer when you
> train. Only with those kinds on, it sends your progress and gains. It never trains,
> switches gym or clicks anything.
