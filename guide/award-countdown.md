> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key.

## What you see

Open **Awards** in Torn. Above the list, one short note names your next award:

- **3 busts to your next award, Bar Breaker. At your pace: Thursday.** The day comes from
  how fast your counters have grown over the last two weeks. A second line names the one
  after it.
- When torn-tracker.com doesn't have your pace yet, the note names the award you're
  closest to earning and how much is left, like **Closest to earning: Bar Breaker, 3 to go.**

Press the award's name to open your awards on torn-tracker.com. With nothing to show, no
note appears.

## Where the numbers come from

They come from the counters torn-tracker.com already keeps for you, the same ones your
dashboard's awards card uses. Nothing new is read from Torn.

## Switching it off

Settings → **Award countdown**.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads nothing on the awards page except where to put the
> note. When the page opens, it asks torn-tracker.com once for your dashboard, with your
> script key, and keeps the answer for a minute. It sends nothing else.
