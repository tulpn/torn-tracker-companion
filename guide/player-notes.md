> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. No key needed.

## Write a note

1. Open the player's profile.
2. Beside their name, press **Add note**.
3. Pick a tag, type your note (up to 300 characters) and press **Save note**.

The tags are **Friend** (green), **Ally** (blue), **Watch** (yellow), **Enemy** (red) and
**Note** (grey). To change a note, press **Edit note**; **Delete** removes it.

## Where you see it

Wherever that player shows up on a page you open:

- on their profile, beside their name;
- in faction member lists and on the ranked-war page, beside their name;
- on the attack page, beside the heading.

The badge shows the tag and the start of the note. Hover over it, or hold it on a phone,
for the whole note. Players without a note get nothing.

## Keep a copy, or move them to another device

In the [settings](settings-panel.md), under **Data**, **Export** puts your notes and your
[racing templates](racing-templates.md) in a box as text. Copy it somewhere safe. On the
other device, paste it into the same box and press **Import**. Importing replaces the
notes that device has.

> **What it reads and sends.** Your notes are kept only on this device, in the
> companion's settings. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no player is looked up for them.
> **Forget me** deletes them, along with everything else the script keeps.
