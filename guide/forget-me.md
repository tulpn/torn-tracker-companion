> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone.

## Clear this device

1. Open the settings (the **Torn Tracker** link in the sidebar) and go to **Data**.
2. Press **Forget me**. The button changes to **Really forget everything?**: press it
   again to confirm.

The companion then forgets your script key, your settings, what it has cached, and
anything still waiting to be sent. It starts again as if freshly installed, with every
feature at its default and every kind of sharing off.

## What it doesn't clear

**Forget me** only clears this device. Your other installs keep their own settings. What
you already shared is on torn-tracker.com: to stop the script key working anywhere,
revoke it on the site's [script key](website:script-key.md) page.
