> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. No key needed.

## Save a template

1. On the racing page, open **Start a custom race** and set it up the way you like: track,
   laps, drivers, upgrades, bet, wait time, password.
2. Above the form, type a name and press **Save these settings**.

You can keep up to ten templates.

## Fill the form from one

Choose a template and press **Fill the form**. The companion sets the form's fields, and
you check them and press Torn's own button: it never starts or creates a race for you. If
Torn's form no longer has a field a template saved, or no longer lists its track, that
field is skipped and the bar says so once.

## Where templates are kept

Only on this device, in the companion's settings. Nothing is sent anywhere. **Forget me**
deletes them, along with everything else the script keeps. Saving your settings or
resetting them leaves your templates alone.
