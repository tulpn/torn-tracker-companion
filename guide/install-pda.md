> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone.

## Add the script from its URL

In Torn PDA's userscripts settings, add a new script **from a URL** and use this one:

```
https://github.com/tulpn/torn-tracker-companion/releases/latest/download/torn-tracker-companion.user.js
```

Don't copy and paste the script's text. A pasted copy can't be updated; one added from
its URL can be checked and kept current.

## Check it works

Open any Torn page in PDA. The round ⚙ button of the [settings panel](settings-panel.md)
appears in the bottom right corner.

## Add your script key

In the panel's **Keys** tab, paste your [script key](website:script-key.md) and press
**Save**. You can use the same key as on your computer: see
[Use it on your other device](second-device.md).
