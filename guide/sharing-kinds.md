> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

Every kind is off until you switch it on. These are the same lines the panel shows beside
each switch.

## Foreign stock

**Sends:** on a foreign shop while you're abroad, the country and each item's stock and
price, empty shelves included. At most once every 30 seconds per country, only while the
tab is on screen.
**Kept and published:** each report with who sent it for 30 days, so the site knows you
were in that country then. Stock and restock times are published as "seen at", never by
whom.

## Loadouts

**Sends:** on an attack, the other player's id and the weapons and armour the attack page
shows them wearing, with damage, accuracy and bonuses. Once per attack.
**Kept and published:** reports for 90 days. Each player's latest loadout is published
openly and removed once nobody has seen it for 180 days. **The player it describes can see
that you reported it.**

## Bazaar listings

**Sends:** on another player's bazaar, their id and each item listed with its quantity and
price, as far as you scroll. At most once per bazaar every five minutes. Never your own
bazaar.
**Kept and published:** reports for 7 days. Each player's latest listings and a price per
item are published.

## Auction listings

**Sends:** on the auction house, each listing you see: the item, the seller (none when
anonymous), the bid and number of bids, the time left, rarity, stats and bonuses. Never the
high bidder. Again only when a bid changes, at most every ten minutes.
**Kept and published:** reports for 90 days. Price history per item and bonus is published.

## Item market prices

**Sends:** on the item market, prices and quantities of up to 50 listings of the item you
opened, never who's selling. Only for items torn-tracker.com asks for right then.
**Kept and published:** reports for 7 days. A price index per item is published.

## Your crime outcomes

**Sends:** when you commit a crime, which crime it was, whether it succeeded, failed or
critically failed, and the money and items you got. Only your own attempts.
**Kept and published:** kept 30 days for your own view. Only totals per crime are
published, once at least 20 players have reported it, never by whom.

## Your gym progress and trains

**Sends:** on the gym page, the gym you're working towards and how far in you are; when you
train, the stat and how much it went up.
**Kept and published:** only for your own gym view, on the site and in the bot. Never shown
to anyone else, never published, not even in totals. Kept 30 days.
