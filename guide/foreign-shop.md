> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key.

## Best buy

On a foreign shop, the item that makes you the most money for what you can still carry
and afford is outlined, with a label like **Best buy: 24 (+$2,000 each, +$48,000)**. Profit
is at torn-tracker.com's market value. Your money and capacity come from the message above
the shelves; without it, the label says "up to". It updates as you buy. You type the amount
and press **Buy** yourself.

## Share the stock you see

If you've switched **Foreign stock** on in [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md), the
companion sends each item's stock and price on the shelves, empty ones included, at most
once every 30 seconds per country. That's what powers restock predictions for everyone.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the shop's shelves and the money and capacity
> message, and asks torn-tracker.com for the items' market values. With Foreign stock on,
> it sends the country, items, stock and prices. The site keeps who sent it for 30 days,
> so it knows you were in that country then; it publishes "seen at", never by whom.

On torn-tracker.com, the [travel planner](website:travel-planner.md) uses this stock to
plan your runs.
