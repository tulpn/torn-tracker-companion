> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. It needs no key.

## What you see

Buyers sometimes mug a seller just after a sale, while the money from it is still on
hand. When you open a sell form carrying more cash than you set, a line above the form
says so:

> You're carrying $2,500,000. Sellers are sometimes mugged just after a sale: bank it
> before you list.

It shows on:

- the Item Market's add-listing form;
- your bazaar's add and manage pages.

It follows the money Torn shows in your sidebar, so it goes away once you've banked the
cash. **Not now** hides it until you next open the page.

Set the amount in the [settings](settings-panel.md): **Wallet reminder**, under **Market
and trading**. It's $1,000,000 to start with.

The reminder only says so. It doesn't move your money or stop you listing.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the cash Torn's sidebar shows you, on the sell
> forms only. It asks nothing and sends nothing.
