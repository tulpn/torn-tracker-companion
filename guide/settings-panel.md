> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone.

## Open the settings

- **In the sidebar:** press **Torn Tracker**. With TornTools installed, it sits right under
  **TornTools Settings**; otherwise it's at the end of **Areas**.
- **By your avatar:** press the small green **TT** icon in Torn's top bar. On a phone or in
  Torn PDA, it's in the header too.
- On a page with neither, a slim **Torn Tracker** tab sits on the right edge of the screen.

The settings open over the page you're on. Nothing is loaded from anywhere to show them.
Close them with **Close**, the **✕**, the **Esc** key, or by pressing outside them.

## Find a setting

Type in **Search settings** at the top to find any switch or option, in any section.
Changes are kept until you press **Save**, so you can move between sections freely; closing
without saving throws them away. The page opens on the section you used last.

## The sections

- **General:** the version, a link to check for an update, and console logging for
  troubleshooting.
- **Features:** every feature, grouped by area (war, travel, market, crimes, gym, jobs),
  each with its switch, one line on what it does, its options, and a **Learn more** link to
  its page here. A feature that's off doesn't run at all. **Reset settings to defaults** is
  at the bottom.
- **Sharing:** a switch for each kind of data the companion can share, every one off to
  start with. See [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md). It also shows what's waiting
  to be sent and, if sharing has stopped, why.
- **Keys:** your torn-tracker.com [script key](website:script-key.md). It isn't a Torn API
  key, it's kept only on this device, and it's never written to the console.
- **Data:** what the companion keeps on this device, and [Forget me](forget-me.md).
- **Credits:** every script the companion took an idea from, with its licence and a link.
- **Help:** this guide, troubleshooting, and where to report a problem.

The page follows Torn's light or dark theme, and on a phone its menu moves to the top.
