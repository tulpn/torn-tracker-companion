**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your script key.

## What you see

On the stock market, beside a stock's name, a label like **far below its 30-day average
($820.5)** or **far above its 30-day average** appears when today's price is well off its
average over the last 30 days. Stocks with nothing unusual, or with fewer than seven days
of closes on torn-tracker.com, show nothing.

## What it is, and isn't

It's a simple signal: it only compares today's price with the last 30 days of closes. It
isn't advice, and it never says buy or sell. The companion never fills or presses Buy or
Sell for you.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads which stocks the page lists and asks
> torn-tracker.com for their signals, the same public market data for everyone. Nothing
> about you is sent or shared.
