> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.
> The dashboard is a preview inside that, switched off by default.

**Core:** free for everyone. Needs your [script key](website:script-key.md).

## Switch it on

1. Open the [settings](settings-panel.md) and go to **General**.
2. Tick **Dashboard (preview)**.
3. It saves at once.

A **Dashboard** link shows at the top of the settings page. It opens the dashboard in
Torn's main column, at `torn.com/index.php#/torn-tracker/dashboard`.

## What you see

One card for each thing torn-tracker.com knows about you, with what needs you now at the
top:

- **Alerts:** your latest alerts from torn-tracker.com, up to ten, each with how long ago
  and a link to its page.
- **Ranked war:** the score while it runs, and **Your dibs** with the time each has left.
  Before it starts, when it starts.
- **Watch rota:** who is on watch now and next, with you marked. An empty slot says
  **Nobody on watch.** You also see your own next watch and how many slots in the next
  24 hours have no Lead. When the chain is running, it shows here too.
- **Chain** and **Organized crime**, the same as the sidebar block shows. The Chain card is
  left out when the chain is already on the Watch rota card.
- **Nearest awards:** the honors and medals you are closest to, with how far you are.
- **Education:** the course you are taking and when it ends, how far you are in its
  branch, and your next pick.
- **Next gym:** how far you are to the next gym. It's an estimate, not Torn's figure.
- **Savings goals**, **Next achievements** and **Getting started**.
- **Money, last 7 days:** income and costs per day, as a chart.

A card with nothing to show is left out. The chart has **Show the numbers** under it, with
the same values as a table. A card ends with **More on torn-tracker.com**, which opens that
card's own page there in a new tab. **Open on torn-tracker.com** at the top opens your full
dashboard.

> **What it reads and sends.** Each time you open the dashboard, the companion asks
> torn-tracker.com once, with your script key, for your dashboard, and keeps the answer
> for a minute. It sends nothing else and asks nothing while the page is closed. It reads
> nothing from Torn's pages for this.

## If it says something else

- **Needs your script key:** add it under **Keys** in the settings.
- **Refused the script key:** check it under **Keys**; it may have been revoked.
- **Asked to wait:** torn-tracker.com limits how often the dashboard is read. Try again
  in a minute.
