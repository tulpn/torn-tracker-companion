> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** free for everyone. Works without a script key.

## What you see

Open **Awards** and the **Merits** tab. When you have free merits:

- A short note above the list says how many you have to spend and names every merit
  they're enough to raise, with what it costs, like **Stealth (1)**. Press a name to
  scroll to that merit.
- Each merit that isn't at its maximum gets a small **next: 3 merits** label. It's green
  when your free merits cover it.

If no merit's next level is within reach yet, the note names the cheapest one instead.
With no free merits, nothing is shown.

## How the cost works

A merit's next level costs one more merit than its current level: going from 0 to 1
costs 1, from 1 to 2 costs 2, and so on. Torn's own text on the tab says the same. Torn
already shows what each merit does, so the hints only add the cost.

## What it never does

It never upgrades a merit, and pressing a name in the note only scrolls the page.
Upgrading is still your own press on the merit in Torn's list.

> **What it reads and sends.** It reads the merits tab you opened: each merit's level and
> your free merits. It sends nothing and asks torn-tracker.com for nothing.
