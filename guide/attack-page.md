> **Test release.** The companion is in public testing. Things may change before version 1.

**Core:** sharing loadouts, and seeing one player's loadout on their profile, are free.
**Pro:** opponents' loadout flags during a ranked war. Sharing needs your script key.

## Share the loadouts you see

When you attack someone, the attack page shows the weapons and armour they're wearing. If
you've switched **Loadouts** on in [Choose what to share](what-is-shared.md), the companion
sends what the page showed, once per attack: their weapons and armour with damage,
accuracy and bonuses. It works whether you win, lose or leave, and never clicks anything.

torn-tracker.com publishes each player's latest loadout openly on their profile. **The
player it describes can see that you reported it.**

> **What it reads and sends.** Only with Loadouts switched on: the other player's id and
> what the attack page shows them wearing. Nothing is sent from your own side.

## A player's loadout on their profile

On anyone's profile, a card shows their latest loadout as torn-tracker.com last saw it:
each weapon and armour piece with damage, accuracy and bonuses, how long ago it was seen
and by how many players, and its danger flags. It's open to everyone, so no key is needed.
It never says who reported it. If the player asked for theirs to be hidden, the card says
so.

## Opponents' flags when you attack (Pro)

When you attack an opponent in your faction's ranked war, their danger flags (heavy
hitter, armoured, temp ready) show under their name. These are the same flags as on the
[war page](war-page.md#opponents-loadouts-pro).

## Your mugs of this player

On a profile or the attack page, a line tells you how often you've mugged this player,
when last and for how much, and what you got on average, for example **You mugged them 3
times: last 2d ago for $1,200,000, $900,000 on average.** It comes from your own records on
torn-tracker.com, so it shows only your mugs. See [Mugger tools](website:mugger-tools.md).

## Fair-fight badge

The attack page also gets a [fair-fight badge](ff-badges.md) beside the player you're
attacking.
