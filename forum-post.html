Torn Tracker Companion
torn-tracker.com and the community's best tools, on the Torn pages you already use.


⚡ Install
Public test (0.x). Things may change before version 1.0, and some features wait on torn-tracker.com or on Torn's page layout. Tell us here what breaks.
  • Browser (Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey): open ▶ the install link and confirm. Your script manager checks for updates by itself.
  • Torn PDA: UserScripts > Add, then add it from this URL (not by pasting the text, which gives a copy PDA can't update):
    https://github.com/tulpn/torn-tracker-companion/releases/latest/download/torn-tracker-companion.user.js
Then open Torn Tracker in Torn's sidebar (under Areas; in the slide-out menu on a phone or in PDA). The settings open as a page inside Torn, with a short tour the first time.

💎 What you see
Every feature has its own switch.
  • Fair-fight badges beside players on profiles, faction pages (the war page too), the attack page and the bounty board, from torn-tracker.com's estimates.
  • War page: sort the opponents and see your faction's dibs; a pre-war strip on the opponent's faction page.
  • Travel: foreign shop stock, the best buy abroad, and your best runs on the travel agency.
  • Trading: a shop price guard on the Item Market and your own sell forms, a price filler that never fills below a shop, trade value, auction value badges.
  • Crimes: morale, value per nerve and burglary targets; weights and success chances for organized crimes; bust safety on the jail page.
  • Attacks: loadouts on profiles and the attack page, and how often you mugged a player.
  • Bounty board: filter by reward, fair fight and level, and see which hospital timers are nearly up.
  • Everyday: gym progress, merit hints, stock signals, racing templates, private player notes and message templates.
  • Your sidebar: a Torn Tracker block under Lists with your chain, war, dibs, med-out partner and OC.
The guide has a page for every feature: torn-tracker.com/guide/companion.


🛡 What it reads and sends
  • It reads only the Torn pages you open yourself: the page, and the replies Torn sends that page (it watches them, never changes or repeats them). It never loads torn.com pages in the background.
  • One click is at most one game action. It fills in fields for you but never submits them, and it never buys, sells, attacks or claims anything.
  • It talks only to torn-tracker.com, with your torn-tracker.com script key (which is not a Torn API key). Features that need the site, like fair-fight badges, need that key; the rest work without it.
  • Sharing starts off. Under Sharing you can choose to send what Torn showed you, kind by kind: foreign stock, auction listings, item market prices, bazaar listings, loadouts, jail difficulty bands, and your own crime outcomes and gym progress. Each switch says exactly what it sends and what torn-tracker.com keeps and publishes; nothing goes until you agree. Nothing is ever sent from company or faction application pages.
  • Every request to torn-tracker.com says the script's version and whether it runs in Torn PDA or a desktop browser. Nothing else about your device.
The code is open on GitHub (GPLv3), with a test suite and a lint that fails the build on anything Torn's scripting rules forbid.
